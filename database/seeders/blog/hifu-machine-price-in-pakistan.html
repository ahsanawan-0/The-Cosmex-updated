What a HIFU machine does, in plain terms

A HIFU handpiece focuses ultrasound energy at a fixed depth under the skin surface, creating small heated points while leaving the surface itself untouched. The depth is set by the cartridge: face cartridges are commonly 1.5 mm, 3.0 mm and 4.5 mm, and body-capable machines add deeper cartridges for body areas. The practitioner chooses cartridges and settings for each area, following training and the manufacturer's protocol.

HIFU 7D vs 9D vs 12D: what is the difference?

"7D", "9D" and "12D" are market names for successive generations of HIFU machines, not a formal technical standard. The differences between two manufacturers' machines can matter more than the number on the front. In general, newer generations tend to add:

  • more cartridges and depths, including body cartridges;
  • faster firing, which shortens treatment time;
  • updated handpieces and software, with larger touch screens.

Running costs: cartridges

Every HIFU cartridge delivers a rated number of shots or lines before it needs replacing. To work out your cost per treatment, divide the cartridge price by its rated shots and multiply by the shots a typical session uses. Ask for these figures before you buy, and confirm that replacement cartridges for your exact machine are available in Pakistan.

HIFU vs RF microneedling (Morpheus 8)

Clients often ask about HIFU and RF microneedling together, and many clinics offer both. They work differently:

  • HIFU focuses ultrasound energy at set depths, with no needles and no break in the skin surface.
  • RF microneedling uses fine needles that deliver radiofrequency energy into the skin, so the needles do pass through the skin surface.

Buying checklist

  1. List the areas you will treat (face only, or face and body) and check the cartridges match.
  2. Get the rated shots per cartridge and the replacement cartridge price.
  3. Compare firing speed and screen or software, not only the "D" number.
  4. Ask what the warranty covers on the handpiece and cartridges.
  5. Confirm installation, operator training and service in Pakistan.
  6. Plan power protection (stabiliser or UPS) for the treatment room.
